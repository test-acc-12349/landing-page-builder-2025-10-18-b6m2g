4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

4.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our Site. They allow us to recognize your device and store information about your preferences and usage patterns. We use both session-based and persistent cookies.

4.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the Site to function properly and enable core features such as security, network management, and accessibility.
  • Performance Cookies: These cookies collect information about how you use our Site, helping us understand which pages are most popular and how visitors navigate the Site.
  • Functional Cookies: These cookies remember your preferences and choices to provide a personalized experience when you return to our Site.
  • Marketing Cookies: These cookies track your activity across websites to display targeted advertising and measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.

4.3 Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to accept, reject, or delete cookies. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our Site and your ability to access certain features. We respect your cookie preferences and will not place non-essential cookies on your device without your consent.

For more information about managing cookies, please visit your browser's help documentation or www.allaboutcookies.org.
