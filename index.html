About LPBuilder

LPBuilder was founded in 2018 by a team of digital marketing experts and software engineers who recognized a critical gap in the market. They saw businesses struggling with expensive landing page builders that required technical expertise, long setup times, and ongoing maintenance costs. Our founders believed that creating high-converting landing pages should be accessible to everyone, regardless of budget or technical skill level. What started as a side project quickly grew into a full-fledged platform serving thousands of businesses worldwide, from solopreneurs to Fortune 500 companies.

Our mission is to democratize digital marketing by providing powerful, affordable tools that help businesses of all sizes create stunning landing pages that convert. We're committed to continuous innovation, exceptional customer service, and building a community where entrepreneurs can learn, grow, and succeed. Every feature we build, every optimization we make, and every decision we take is guided by our core values: simplicity, affordability, reliability, and customer success. We believe that great marketing shouldn't require a marketing degree or a massive budget—it should be available to anyone with a vision and determination to succeed.
